## Key Thematic Insights

### 1. Pull-to-Center (PTC) Effect
The most robust finding across 25+ years of experimental research: human inventory decision-makers systematically **under-order** high-profit products (critical fractile > 0.5) and **over-order** low-profit products (critical fractile < 0.5), pulling orders toward mean demand μ.

### 3. Core Cognitive Mechanisms Identified
| Mechanism | Description | Key Papers |
|-----------|-------------|------------|
| **Anchoring & Insufficient Adjustment** | Mean demand acts as default cognitive anchor | Schweitzer & Cachon (2000), Gavirneni & Xia (2009) |
| **Demand Chasing** | Reactive adjustment: Q_t = f(D_{t-1}) | Lau & Bearden (2013), Kirshner & Moritz (2021) |
| **Ex-Post Inventory Error Minimization** | Disutility from |Q - D| mismatch | Schweitzer & Cachon (2000) |
| **Mental Accounting** | Payment scheme framing alters perceived costs | Chen et al. (2013), Gu & Zhang (2020) |
| **Loss Aversion / Prospect Theory** | Asymmetric valuation of gains vs. losses | Ho et al. (2010), Long & Nasiry (2015) |
| **Observation/Censoring Bias** | Unobservable lost sales distort learning | Rudi & Drake (2014), Feiler et al. (2013) |
| **Overconfidence** | Overprecision in demand variance estimation | Ren & Croson (2013) |
| **Supply Line Underweighting** | Neglect of on-order inventory | Sterman (1989), Croson & Donohue (2006) |

### 4. Debiasing Mechanisms
| Intervention | Effect | Key Papers |
|-------------|--------|------------|
| Structured feedback (foregone payoffs) | Reduces PTC moderately | Bolton & Katok (2008) |
| Standing orders & commitment devices | Reduces adjustment frequency bias | Bolton & Katok (2008) |
| Behavioral contract calibration | Restores channel efficiency | Becker-Peth et al. (2013) |
| Goal framing (gain vs. loss vs. neutral) | Shifts safety stock decisions | Kosgoda et al. (2024), Tokar et al. (2016) |
| REDO estimation nudges | Mitigates censoring bias | Tong et al. (2018) |
| Extensive practice (100+ rounds) | Substantially reduces PTC | Brokesova et al. (2025) |

### 5. External Validity
- **Bolton et al. (2012):** Professional procurement managers exhibit identical biases as student subjects
- **van Donselaar et al. (2010):** Retail store managers override automated ERP replenishment with biased adjustments
- **Brokesova et al. (2022):** Pull-to-center is a general property of asymmetric payoff tasks, not inventory-specific
